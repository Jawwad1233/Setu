# Bengali source notes

The requested [Scribd document](https://www.scribd.com/document/689677087/Bengali-Slang-Words-With-Meaning-Bengali) was checked on 2026-09-26. Its accessible text exposed repeated profile links and the linked preview images failed to load. No definitions from that document were imported. A readable PDF or pasted text is needed to use that particular source.

The starter glossary uses short paraphrased senses from the sources linked per entry. Example sentences and translations were authored for this project; they are not quotations. Newspaper observations are not a comprehensive or independently validated slang dictionary. Literal and regional meanings coexist. No claim is made that all terms are new or exclusive to Bangladesh.
